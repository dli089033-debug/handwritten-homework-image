# 生成前后验收清单

Read this before final delivery. Verify content first, then presentation.

## Content fidelity

- [ ] Only explicitly assigned questions are present.
- [ ] No required question is missing.
- [ ] Every black-ink answer matches the approved draft, including planned mistakes.
- [ ] The realized correctness rate is stated honestly.
- [ ] Red corrections, if any, fix the actual wrong step.
- [ ] No red correction appears at 100% correctness.

## Writing behavior

- [ ] The page reads as one continuous first attempt.
- [ ] No artificial blank zones were reserved for red corrections.
- [ ] Notation is student-appropriate and no full question text is copied unnecessarily.
- [ ] Character forms, slant, spacing, and alignment vary naturally.
- [ ] Equations and numbers remain legible at mobile size.

## Page and camera

- [ ] The work fits comfortably on the intended page count.
- [ ] Paper is not pure digital white and is not heavily aged.
- [ ] Perspective and shadows are mild and plausible.
- [ ] Props do not cover the work.
- [ ] The result does not look like a scan, perfect overhead studio shot, or typeset worksheet.

## Inspection procedure

1. Inspect the full-resolution image for question and answer fidelity.
2. Inspect a reduced mobile-size preview for readability and composition.
3. Compare every intentional mistake and red correction against the approved plan.
4. Regenerate or locally revise any material defect; do not merely describe it.
5. Deliver with a short AI-simulation disclosure.
