# 图像生成提示词模板

Use this as a composition aid. Replace bracketed fields with verified content; never pass brackets or unsupported claims into the final prompt.

## Main prompt

Create an AI-simulated casual smartphone photograph of completed student homework on ordinary A4 paper.

Content requirements:

- Include only these assigned questions: [QUESTION NUMBERS].
- Write exactly this verified black-ink work, in order: [BLACK-INK DRAFT].
- Target correctness: [TARGET RATE].
- Intentional mistakes: [ERROR PLAN OR NONE].
- Add these red-pen corrections only at the corresponding mistaken steps: [RED CORRECTIONS OR NONE].
- Do not copy full question statements and do not add a title unless required.

Handwriting direction:

- [REFERENCE-DERIVED TRAITS OR ORDINARY IMPERFECT STUDENT HANDWRITING].
- Vary repeated digits subtly; allow small baseline, spacing, and slant changes.
- Keep the work compact and readable, as if written continuously without reserving correction space.

Paper and photography:

- slightly warm off-white A4 paper with restrained natural creases;
- casual desk setting, slight rotation and perspective, soft natural shadow;
- deep gray-black or blue-black ink with mild pressure variation;
- red gel pen only where corrections are required;
- optional small edge fragments of a laptop, textbook, scratch paper, or pen;
- the entire written area must remain visible and legible.

Avoid scanner-flat rendering, perfect typography, repeated identical glyphs, excessive cleanliness, dramatic props, strong blur, or any text not listed above.

## Negative checks

Reject and regenerate if the image introduces:

- extra questions, missing questions, or altered answers;
- a heading that was not requested;
- red marks at 100% correctness;
- pre-planned blank correction zones;
- perfectly repeated characters or typeset equations;
- cropped, covered, or unreadable work;
- a claim or watermark suggesting authentic human handwriting or a real photograph.
