# 字迹、纸张与拍摄规则

Use this reference only when creating or revising the final visual.

## Handwriting

When a reference is supplied, study broad visible traits:

- numeral and Latin-letter shapes;
- fraction bars, equals signs, parentheses, and side-by-side expressions;
- question-number style;
- size, slant, baseline drift, line spacing, and correction habits.

Keep natural variation. Repeated digits should not be identical, baselines should not be perfectly horizontal, and spacing may tighten or loosen. Allow mild crowding and small alignment errors without destroying legibility.

## Page composition

- Default to ordinary A4 paper.
- No decorative title unless the assignment explicitly requires one.
- Keep question numbers compact and content-dense.
- Prefer one page when the work remains readable.
- Avoid mechanical alignment, perfectly even margins, and uniformly distributed lines.

## Ink

Black work:

- deep gray-black or blue-black;
- slight pressure and stroke-width variation;
- never perfectly uniform digital black.

Red corrections:

- realistic red gel-pen appearance;
- slightly smaller, tighter, or more slanted than the black writing;
- anchored to the actual mistaken step;
- absent when correctness is 100%.

## Paper

- off-white rather than pure white;
- optional light folds, dents, tiny wrinkles, or softly curled edges;
- no heavy aging, stains, or theatrical damage.

## Phone-photo look

Aim for a casual desk photograph taken after the work is finished:

- slight rotation and mild perspective;
- natural, soft shadowing;
- restrained desk context such as part of a laptop, textbook, scratch paper, or pen;
- page remains the clear subject and all answers remain readable.

Avoid:

- scanner-flat texture;
- perfectly centered studio overhead photography;
- excessive blur depth of field;
- pristine staged minimalism;
- dramatic cinematic lighting;
- props covering any answer.
